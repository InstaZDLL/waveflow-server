WaveFlow Server for Windows
===========================

Your own music server, on this computer. Nothing to install.

To start it
-----------

1. If you are looking at this file inside the zip, extract the zip first:
   right-click it, "Extract All". The server cannot find its tools from inside
   a zip.

2. Double-click waveflow-server.exe.

   The first time, Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" and call the
   publisher unknown: this download is signed, but with a certificate of our
   own, which Windows does not know. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

   A window opens, and your web browser opens on the server's page.

To stop it, close that window. To start it again, double-click
waveflow-server.exe again: your music and your account are still there.
