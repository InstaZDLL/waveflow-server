WaveFlow Server for macOS
=========================

Your own music server, on this computer. Nothing to install.

To start it
-----------

1. Double-click waveflow-server.

   The first time, macOS refuses to open it, because this download is not
   signed yet. It refuses each of the three programs in this folder in turn:
   waveflow-server, ffmpeg and ffprobe. There are two ways past that.

   The short way, once for the whole folder: open Terminal, type the line
   below followed by a space, drag this folder onto the Terminal window, and
   press Return.

       xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine

   The long way, without Terminal: after each refusal, click "Done", open
   System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down to Security and click
   "Open Anyway" beside the program's name, then confirm with your password.
   Then double-click waveflow-server again. The server stops with an error
   naming ffmpeg or ffprobe while one of them is still refused.

2. A Terminal window opens, and your web browser opens on the server's page.

To stop it, close that Terminal window. To start it again, double-click
waveflow-server again: your music and your account are still there.
